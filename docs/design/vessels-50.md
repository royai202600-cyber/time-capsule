# 50 new vessels: plan (for Roy's OK)

Status: **proposal**. No art is made until Roy approves this list and the first batch. Every vessel follows [design/art/VESSEL-SOP.md](../../design/art/VESSEL-SOP.md).

## The idea in one line
Each vessel is an **object people already have feelings about**: a mixtape, a ramen bowl, a red packet, a snow globe. Sending one says something before the letter is even opened. They're inspired by pop-culture moods and tropes, but every design is our own.

## Why people will love them
- **Emotional hook:** each object carries a memory (a mixtape for a crush, a ramen bowl after a bad day, a red packet from grandma).
- **Shareable:** the opening animation is a little "moment" worth a screen recording (a fortune cookie cracking, snow settling, a phone-booth door swinging open).
- **Collectable:** the Cabinet fills up, seasonal pieces come back once a year, and numbered editions make people want the low numbers.
- **The right one for every occasion:** birthdays, anniversaries, graduation, apology, future self, long-distance, new baby, memorial and each festival all have a vessel.

## Pop culture without borrowing anyone's property
We evoke a **mood or trope**, never a character, logo, name or signature design. No Harry Potter, Ghibli, Disney, Pokémon, Marvel, Nintendo, Sanrio or Doctor Who items. Ideas I rejected or changed:

| First idea | Problem | Replaced with |
|---|---|---|
| Glass slipper | Cinderella's slipper as Disney designed it | **Midnight pocket watch** (#44): the same "before the clock strikes twelve" feeling |
| Handheld game console | The classic handheld's shape is protected trade dress | **Mini arcade cabinet** (#6), an original shape |
| Virtual-pet egg toy | Too close to a branded toy | **Gashapon capsule** (#48) |
| Owl carrying a letter | Too close to the wizard-school films | **Paper plane** (#23) |
| Blue police box | Trademarked TV icon | **Red phone booth** (#32), a generic design without the crown or "TELEPHONE" sign |
| Red-and-white ball capsule | Looks like a Poké Ball | The gashapon is pastel, half clear, with no centre band or button |
| "Polaroid" | Brand name and rainbow-stripe trade dress | "Instant camera" (#4), plain body, no stripe |
| Genie lamp with a genie | The Aladdin film's look | Plain brass oil lamp with wishing smoke (#41), no genie figure |

**Rule for every prompt:** "original design, no logos, no brand shapes, no characters". Before merge, the reviewer checks each image for resemblance to existing IP.

## Opening types
Codes used in the table. Full rules are in the SOP.

- Already built in the app:
  - **A** hinge lid (tin, music box, ring box)
  - **B** lift-off cover (dome glass)
  - **C** cap lift (lantern)
  - **D** pop and pull (bottle cork, then the letter slides out)
  - **E** flap fold (envelope: the seal fades, the flap folds)
- New, built once in code and then reusable:
  - **F** slide out (a sleeve, drawer or photo slides out)
  - **G** unscrew (a lid twists and lifts)
  - **H** split (two halves swing apart)
  - **J** door swing (a door turns on a side hinge)
  - **K** pop-up (the inside springs up)

Idle motions: sway, bob, breathe, glow, drift (steam, snow, fireflies), spin, tick, plus a sparkle or glint point.

Tiers:
- **Free**
- **Shop**: price band MOP 6–28 (free while testing)
- **Limited**: seasonal or numbered

★ = proposed first batch (10).

Privacy Policy

我們想讓你清楚知道時光膠囊會保存甚麼、甚麼時候使用位置，以及誰可以打開你的信。

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026/11/07 · 營運者 / Operator: Admin

繁體中文

我們保存的資料

帳戶電郵、姓名、所選頭像、可選填的城市及提醒設定；好友關係與邀請；膠囊的寄件人、收件人、容器、提示、封存和預定開啟時間，以及可選的開啟地點。我們也保存你寄出的信件文字或手寫圖像及可選的照片。容器擁有紀錄和示範品牌的禮物領取碼也可能與膠囊一同保存。

封存與分享

收件人可在開啟前看到容器、提示及開啟條件；信件和照片在到達指定時間及地點（如有）前，不會在任何應用程式中顯示給任何人，包括寄件人。只有收件人能透過應用程式打開內容。這是應用程式和資料庫權限的限制；營運者在技術上仍可存取資料庫，包括封存內容。

位置資料

有指定地點的膠囊需要位置權限。應用程式只在開啟期間讀取位置，用來顯示距離和檢查能否打開膠囊；不會在背景追蹤位置。開啟膠囊時，伺服器會核對位置及距離，並保存開啟時間、開啟位置及與指定地點的距離。沒有指定地點的膠囊不需要位置。

服務供應商

Supabase 在東京地區提供帳戶驗證及資料庫；Vercel 託管網站；Google 是可選的登入方式。地圖及導航可能使用 OpenStreetMap、Apple Maps 或 Google Maps；使用這些服務時，它們可能收到你的連線或導航資料。

刪除帳戶及聯絡我們

目前請電郵 info@docai.net 申請刪除帳戶及相關資料。我們會核實申請並回覆。應用程式內刪除功能仍在準備中。私隱問題亦可寄到同一地址。

本服務並非為 13 歲以下兒童設計。AI 不會代寫信件；日後可能只用來建議有意義的地點。

ENGLISH

What we store

Your sign-in email, name, chosen portrait, optional city and reminder preferences; friendships and invites; and each capsule’s sender, recipient, vessel, hint, sealing and scheduled opening times, and optional place. We also store the typed or handwritten letter and any attached photo. Vessel ownership and a demo brand gift pickup code may be stored with a capsule.

Sealed content and sharing

Before opening, the recipient can see the vessel, hint and opening conditions. No app shows the letter or photo to anyone, including the sender, until the set time and place (if any). Only the recipient can open the content through the app. These are app and database access rules: the operator could technically access the database, including sealed content.

Location

A place-locked capsule needs location permission. The app reads location only while it is open, to show distance and check whether the capsule can open. It does not track location in the background. At opening, the server checks location and distance, then stores the opening time, location and distance from the set place. A capsule without a place does not need location.

Service providers

Supabase handles authentication and the database in its Tokyo region. Vercel hosts the website. Google sign-in is optional. Maps and navigation may use OpenStreetMap, Apple Maps or Google Maps; those services may receive connection or navigation data when you use them.

Account deletion and contact

For now, email info@docai.net to request deletion of your account and related data. We will verify and respond to the request. In-app deletion is still being prepared. Use the same address for privacy questions.

The service is not directed at children under 13. AI does not write letters; it may later suggest meaningful places.

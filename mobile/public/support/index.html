Support

登入、開啟膠囊或管理帳戶遇到問題？我們在這裡協助。

聯絡電郵 / Contact email: info@docai.net

繁體中文

怎樣聯絡我們？

請電郵 info@docai.net，說明你遇到的問題。帳戶問題請用註冊電郵聯絡；不要在郵件中傳送密碼或信件內容。

為甚麼打不開膠囊？

先檢查開啟時間是否已到。如膠囊設有地點，請允許應用程式在開啟期間使用位置，並在指定地點 100 米內等待定位變得準確。伺服器會再次核對時間與位置。

如何刪除帳戶？

目前請電郵 info@docai.net 申請，我們會核實及回覆。應用程式內刪除功能尚未提供。

提醒開關現時只保存你的偏好；通知功能尚未推出。商店在測試期間免費，不會收取款項。Maison Aurèle 及其禮物是示範內容。

ENGLISH

How do I contact you?

Email info@docai.net and describe the problem. For account issues, write from your sign-in email. Do not send passwords or letter contents in your message.

Why can’t I open a capsule?

Check that its opening time has passed. If it has a place, allow location access while the app is open, stand within 100 metres of the place and wait for an accurate location. The server checks the time and location again.

How do I delete my account?

For now, email info@docai.net to request deletion. We will verify and respond. In-app deletion is not available yet.

Reminder switches currently save your preferences; notifications have not launched. The shop is free during testing and does not collect payments. Maison Aurèle and its gifts are demos.

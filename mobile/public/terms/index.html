寄出與開啟

你可以寄膠囊給自己或好友。寄出後，寄件人不能在應用程式內閱讀或修改封存內容。收件人要等到指定時間；如設定了地點，還須在該地點的 100 米範圍內，並提供足夠準確的位置，才可開啟。位置由伺服器再次核對。請在寄出前確認收件人、內容、時間及地點。

你寄出的內容

請只寄送你有權分享的文字、手寫內容及照片，並尊重收件人。不要用本服務傳送違法、威脅或侵害他人的內容。膠囊中的文字由你撰寫；AI 不會代寫。日後 AI 可能提供地點建議。

測試中的商店與示範品牌

測試期間容器可免費取得，平台不收取付款。顯示的價格不是目前的收費。Maison Aurèle 是虛構的示範品牌；其精品店、禮物及領取碼都是示範內容，不能兌換真實商品。

帳戶與協助

本服務並非為 13 歲以下兒童設計。你可以在 私隱政策 了解資料使用及刪除帳戶的方法。如遇到問題，請到 支援頁 或電郵 info@docai.net。

ENGLISH

Sending and opening

You can send a capsule to yourself or a friend. After sending, the sender cannot read or edit its sealed contents in the app. The recipient must wait until the set time. If a place is set, they must also be within 100 metres of it and provide a sufficiently accurate location. The server checks that location again. Please check the recipient, content, time and place before sending.

What you send

Send only text, handwriting and photos you have the right to share, and respect the recipient. Do not use the service to send unlawful, threatening or harmful content. You write the letter yourself; AI does not write it. AI may suggest places later.

Testing shop and demo brand

Vessels are free to acquire during testing, and no payments are collected. Displayed prices are not current charges. Maison Aurèle is a fictional demo brand. Its boutique, gifts and pickup codes are for demonstration and cannot be redeemed for real products.

Account and help

The service is not directed at children under 13. See the Privacy Policy for data use and account deletion. For help, visit Support or email info@docai.net.
